### 1. Executive Bonus Structure
Annual performance bonuses for VP and C-Suite executives are calculated according to the Corporate Performance Multiplier (CPM):
- **Target Bonus Percentage:** 40% of base salary for Vice Presidents; 60% of base salary for C-Level executives.
- **EBITDA Milestone Gate:** No bonuses will be paid out if corporate annual EBITDA falls below 90% of the target board forecast.
- **Payout Schedule:** 50% paid in cash in Q1 following the audited fiscal report, and 50% granted as Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) with a 1-year cliff.

### 2. Standard Engineering & Product Salary Bands (US / Remote Tier 1)
All base salaries are benchmarked against the 75th percentile of Radford Technology Survey data:
- **L3 (Software Engineer I):** $115,000 – $145,000 base + 10,000 ISO options.
- **L4 (Software Engineer II):** $145,000 – $185,000 base + 25,000 ISO options.
- **L5 (Senior Software Engineer):** $185,000 – $235,000 base + 45,000 ISO options.
- **L6 (Staff / Principal Engineer):** $235,000 – $310,000 base + 75,000 ISO options.

### 3. Equity Vesting Schedules & Acceleration
- Standard employee equity grants follow a **4-year vesting schedule with a 12-month cliff (25% at month 12, then 1/36th monthly thereafter)**.
- **Double-Trigger Acceleration:** For VP and Director level employees, unvested equity features double-trigger acceleration upon a Change of Control combined with termination without cause within 12 months of the acquisition.

### 4. Discretionary Retention Grants
Department heads may request out-of-cycle retention grants for critical contributors whose unvested equity value has diminished by > 40% due to valuation adjustments. Such grants require dual written approval from the Chief People Officer (CPO) and Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
