# Global Employee Travel, Expense Reimbursement, and Per Diem Handbook

**Document ID:** FIN-EXP-2026-ALL  
**Department:** Finance, Operations  
**Access Level:** `all` (Public to all internal staff)  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  

---

### 1. General Travel Policy
All business travel must be booked via our corporate travel portal (Navan) at least 14 days in advance for domestic travel and 21 days in advance for international flights. 

### 2. Airfare Classes & Approvals
- Flights under 6 hours: Economy / Main Cabin only.
- Flights exceeding 6 consecutive hours (transcontinental or international): Premium Economy is permitted.
- Business Class is strictly reserved for flights over 10 hours with mandatory next-day client engagements, subject to Department VP pre-approval.

### 3. Daily Per Diem & Meals
Employees traveling on business are entitled to reimbursement for meals and incidentals according to the destination city tier:
- **Tier 1 Cities (New York, SF, London, Tokyo, Singapore):** Up to $110 USD per day.
- **Tier 2 Cities (Austin, Berlin, Toronto, Bangalore):** Up to $80 USD per day.
- **Standard Locations:** Up to $65 USD per day.
Alcohol is reimbursable only during pre-approved client dinners, capped at two drinks per team member.

### 4. Expense Submission Deadlines & Receipts
- Itemized receipts are required for all individual expenses exceeding $25 USD.
- Credit card summary slips that do not show individual items are not accepted by accounting.
- All expense reports must be submitted through Expensify within **30 calendar days** of the expense occurrence. Reports submitted past 60 days will be rejected and non-reimbursable.
- Home office internet stipend ($75/month) does not require itemized receipts once approved by HR during onboarding.
